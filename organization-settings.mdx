---
title: "Organization settings: view team members and roles"
description: "Use the Organization settings panel in the PlantOPS admin app to see every team member in your organization with their role and account status."
---

The **Organization settings** panel shows the people in your organization and the state of their accounts. Use it when you need to confirm who has access to the admin app, what role each person holds, or whether an account is active.

## Where to find it

The PlantOPS admin app displays the Organization settings panel at the top of the main application layout. You see it on every page after you open the admin app, so you don't need to navigate to a separate route.

## Team member table

The panel lists each team member in a table with the following columns:

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The team member's full name. |
| **Role** | The team member's role in the organization, such as `Admin`. |
| **Status** | The team member's account status, such as `Active`. |

For example, an organization with a single administrator shows one row:

| Name | Role | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mohamed Riyanudeen | Admin | Active |

<Note>
  The panel is read-only. You can't add, remove, or edit team members from this table.
</Note>
